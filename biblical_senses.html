The Biblical Principle Foundation

Training to Distinguish Good from Evil

The author of Hebrews contrasts infancy with by comparing milk with solid food. Just as an infant's enzymes must develop, moral does not happen automatically—it is forged "by constant use" and daily choices.

⚛️ Neurobiology Bridge: Peripheral Adaptation vs. Central Synaptic Plasticity (LTP)

In neurophysiology, peripheral sensory receptors adjust rapidly to steady backgrounds. By contrast, moral formation operates via central plasticity: repeated deliberate choices trigger Long-Term Potentiation (LTP) via NMDA/AMPA glutamate receptors in the prefrontal cortex, strengthening pathways of self-control—whereas repeated passive exposure to harmful stimuli the through emotional habituation.

The Connection: Physical Perception → Ethical Discernment

  • Physical senses decode environmental energy (photons, pressure, chemicals) to keep the body physically safe.
  • Spiritual senses evaluate ideas, relationships, and media through truth to protect character and purpose.
